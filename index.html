Questions

Before you join.

Does it cost anything?

No. Migrant is free for everyone who joins. There is no subscription and no fee to use it.

How does Migrant earn money?

Local businesses, PGs and employers pay to reach people on Migrant, and local guides share in the revenue their area creates. Newcomers never pay.

How do you keep listings and reviews honest?

Places are checked before they are marked verified, reviews come from verified members who have used the place, and local guides moderate each area.

Which areas do you cover?

We start with Koramangala and HSR Layout in Bangalore, then expand to other areas and cities based on demand.

What happens to my details?

We collect only what is needed to contact you and set up your area. We do not sell your information, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.
